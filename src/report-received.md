## What happens next

1. **We try to reproduce it** from the steps and screenshots you sent.
2. **We contact you if anything needs clarifying**, but only if you left an email. Your submission ID is in the confirmation email.
3. **It goes on the [injection list](/injections/)** once it meets the [listing criteria](/about/#what-gets-listed), credited the way you asked.

## If it's unpatched

**Don't publish it yourself** until the vendor has shipped a fix. If you haven't told the vendor yet, do. Most have a security contact or a `security.txt` file.
